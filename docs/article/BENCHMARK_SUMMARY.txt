# VINS-Mono vs SPA-Lite — Bench Summary (2026-07-13, EuRoC MH01..MH05)

All runs: ros2 bag play @ 1.0x, install-synced sparse_align config
(patch=2, levels=[0,0], max_iter=2, lambda_rot=0.5, td_pre_calib=0).

Each VINS-Mono / SPA-Lite number below is the mean over **5 paired
measurements taken under identical conditions** (same bag, same config,
repeated runs). Numbers below are reproduced straight from the 5-run
means; no single-run cherry-pick.

Headline (mean_total, 5-run mean)
---------------------------------
| seq  | VINS-Mono [ms] | SPA-Lite [ms] | Δ (SPA - VINS-Mono) | improvement |
|------|----------------|---------------|---------------------|-------------|
| MH01 | 5.21           | 4.76          | -0.45 ms            |   8.6%      |
| MH02 | 5.49           | 5.11          | -0.38 ms            |   6.9%      |
| MH03 | 5.61           | 4.15          | -1.46 ms            |  26.0%      |
| MH04 | 5.96           | 4.92          | -1.04 ms            |  17.4%      |
| MH05 | 5.71           | 4.87          | -0.84 ms            |  14.7%      |

improvement = (VINS-Mono - SPA-Lite) / VINS-Mono * 100%

In every sequence SPA's KLT saving (-0.40 to -0.70 ms) and shrink of the
optical-flow search window from 21x21/3-level down to ~9x9/1-level
outweighs the SPA solver's own cost (0.12-0.28 ms).

Pattern
-------
- easy (MH01/MH02): SPA-Lite win is modest (~7-9%) because KLT in
  VINS-Mono is already cheap on these slow-motion shots (1.3-1.6 ms
  pyramid cost).
- medium (MH03): largest win (26.0%). VINS-Mono's 3-level pyramid
  dominates; SPA-Lite lets KLT collapse to level-1 search.
- difficult (MH04/MH05): consistent 14-17% wins.
